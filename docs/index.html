Everything for daily bookkeeping.

Monee keeps the core flows close to the user: record quickly, review budgets, plan recurring spending, and see useful information on the home screen.

01

Bookkeeping

Track income and expenses across books, accounts, categories, payment methods, notes, and tags.

02

Budgets and plans

Manage monthly budgets, category budgets, saving goals, scheduled ledger entries, and reminders.

03

Auto Ledger

Use App Shortcuts, Vision OCR, local parsing, and optional OpenAI-compatible parsing for screenshots.

04

Widgets

Surface today spending, budget progress, quick actions, account overview, and Auto Ledger status.

Built as a privacy-first native app.

The app centers state in LedgerStore, keeps models serializable with Codable, and publishes compact widget snapshots instead of exposing the full app state to extensions.
